# Product Vision

This document defines the problem Make Dating Free Again addresses, the people it serves, and the outcome it seeks. It
does not define product rules or claim that proposed solutions have been validated.

## Mission

Help adults form meaningful, mutual connections with less dating-app fatigue and less unnecessary effort.

The desired outcome is progress toward a connection that the people involved consider worthwhile, not permanent use of
the product.

## Problem

Many dating-app experiences require people to evaluate large numbers of profiles through rapid, repetitive decisions.
Users may encounter:

- choice overload and decision fatigue;
- repeated rejection without a sense of progress;
- unclear dating intentions and low-quality interactions;
- distrust in opaque recommendations;
- emotional exhaustion and compulsive usage patterns;
- a gap between the number of matches and meaningful dating outcomes.

A Match alone does not resolve these problems. People still need enough mutual understanding to decide whether to
continue the connection and, if both want to, meet safely outside the product.

The empirical support and limits of these claims are documented in [research.md](./research.md).

## Who the Product Is For

The product is initially for adults who:

- seek a mutual connection rather than continuous profile browsing;
- experience choice overload or repeated low-quality interactions in existing dating products;
- are willing to state their Dating Intent and consider Recommendations deliberately;
- value transparency and control over their time and attention.

The product does not assume that every User wants the same kind of relationship. Supported Dating Intents, audiences,
and markets must be defined through dedicated research and feature specifications.

## Intended Outcome

Make Dating Free Again seeks to help Users move from a relevant Recommendation to Mutual Interest, a Meaningful
Conversation, and, when both people choose it, a safe Offline Meeting.

This is a direction, not a promise. The product cannot guarantee compatibility, a relationship, or an Offline Meeting.
Canonical meanings for these outcomes are defined in [glossary.md](./glossary.md).

## Current Stage

The project is under development. It is testing product and technical hypotheses, and most capabilities described by
the vision have not yet been implemented or validated with product data.

The beliefs behind the desired product are documented in [philosophy.md](./philosophy.md). Operational product decisions
must follow [principles.md](./principles.md).
