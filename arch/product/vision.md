# Product Vision

This document defines the problem Make Dating Free Again addresses, the people it serves, and the outcome it seeks. It
does not define detailed product rules or claim that the current product hypothesis has been validated.

## Mission

Help adults form meaningful, mutual connections with less dating-app fatigue and less unnecessary effort.

The desired outcome is progress toward a connection that the people involved consider worthwhile, not permanent use of
the product.

## Problem

Finding a worthwhile conversation in existing dating products can feel more like an exception than a predictable
outcome. Four related patterns may contribute:

1. **Choice overload:** Users may see too many Dating Profiles, evaluate people superficially, and move rapidly to the
   next option.
2. **Asynchronous communication friction:** The path from initial interest to a real conversation may involve waiting,
   repeated text exchanges, ghosting, and loss of interest.
3. **Decision fatigue:** Users repeatedly decide whether to view, decline, express interest, write, answer, and continue,
   without a clear sense of completion.
4. **Use for emotional regulation:** Some usage is associated with coping, mood enhancement, boredom relief, or social
   connection rather than deliberate progress toward meeting someone. Endless supply and retention mechanics may
   reinforce unproductive use, but the current evidence does not establish that causal effect.

These patterns can produce many interactions or app-defined matches without enough mutual understanding to make an
informed next decision. The empirical support, limitations, and unresolved claims are documented in
[research.md](./research.md).

## Who the Product Is For

The product is initially for adults who:

- seek a mutual connection rather than continuous Dating Profile browsing;
- experience choice overload or repeated low-quality interactions in existing dating products;
- are willing to state their Dating Intent and Boundaries and consider Recommendations deliberately;
- value transparency and control over their time, attention, personal information, and participation.

The product does not assume that every User wants the same kind of relationship. Supported Dating Intents, audiences,
markets, and accessibility needs require dedicated research and feature specifications.

## Initial Pilot Audience

The initial pilot narrows that broader audience to adults who are willing and able to test a short synchronous
conversation before persistent communication. This is a recruitment condition for testing the current product
hypothesis, not a permanent definition of whom the product may serve.

The pilot must investigate who is excluded or disadvantaged by synchronous participation and whether voice, video, or
another supported mode is sufficiently comfortable, accessible, and safe.

## Current Product Hypothesis

The product is testing whether a shorter path from limited, deliberate choice to synchronous voice or video
communication helps Users make a better-informed decision with less avoidable effort.

The hypothesized journey is:

```text
Dating Intent and Boundaries
  -> Live Availability
  -> Recommendation
  -> Preview Interest
  -> Mutual Preview Interest
  -> Conversation Pairing
  -> Live Conversation
  -> Continue Decision
  -> Match
  -> Post-Match Communication
  -> optional Offline Meeting
```

Preview Interest means only willingness to try a short Live Conversation. It is not a Match or consent to later
contact. A Match is created only after an eligible Live Conversation when both Users independently decide to continue.
The effectiveness, acceptable format, timing, and safety of this journey remain hypotheses. It is also unresolved
whether a Recommendation requires both Users to remain simultaneously live-available or whether Preview Interest may
persist across availability windows.

## Intended Outcome

Make Dating Free Again seeks to help a User move from limited and intentional consideration to a short Live
Conversation, then make an informed and voluntary Continue Decision about the connection.

A Meaningful Conversation, informed non-continuation, a Match, or a consensually reported Offline Meeting may each be
a Meaningful Outcome. The product does not guarantee:

- compatibility;
- a Match;
- a relationship;
- an Offline Meeting;
- personal safety.

The project is under development. It is testing product and technical hypotheses, and most capabilities described by
the vision have not yet been implemented or validated with product data.
